# AET Blog Article Structure

Keep articles focused on a real customer question. Use this short format for new
articles and requested editorial revisions.

1. **Search-focused title.** Use one clear H1 with the main topic in natural
   language, preferably a customer question. Add a descriptive URL and a concise
   SEO description; avoid keyword repetition.
2. **Answer first.** Open with a direct two- or three-sentence answer, then explain
   the conditions and details in the body.
3. **AI links near the top.** After the introduction, before the contents or main
   sections, add an “Explore this article with AI” row. Include working links to
   ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Claude, and Grok where supported. Prefill
   the reader's question and the article's public canonical URL when supported;
   verify each destination rather than assuming every platform accepts the same
   link format. Use distinct platform labels for click reporting.
4. **Questions in the reader's voice.** Prefer first-person H2 headings such as
   “Do I need a credential evaluation?”, “What documents do I need?”, and
   “How much will it cost?”. Give the answer immediately below each question.
   Use H3 only for useful subdivisions; do not force every heading into a question.
5. **Easy-to-scan answers.** Use short paragraphs, document checklists, numbered
   steps, and tables for actual comparisons. Link official sources beside key
   requirements. Use clear section anchors and image descriptions. Include only
   supported prices, dates, authorship, and service claims.
6. **A useful ending.** Add a few remaining FAQs when needed, then a phone,
   Contact-page, or application CTA; do not use direct email CTAs. Avoid repeating
   the same questions or sales pitch.

## Example outline

- H1: Can I use my foreign degree for a California contractor license?
- Short answer / introduction
- Explore this article with AI: platform links
- Contents, if the article is long
- H2: Do I need a credential evaluation?
- H2: What documents do I need?
- H2: How do I confirm the right report type?
- H2: How much will it cost, and how long will it take?
- Additional questions, official sources, and contact link

Question headings are an editorial preference, not a search-engine requirement.
AI links help readers explore the article; they do not guarantee indexing or AI
recommendations. Existing language and visual rules stay in [Design](design.md);
legacy-source handling stays in the [Blog README](../content/blog/README.md).
